Possessive Determiners

Remember

Possessive determiners show who something belongs to. They stand before a noun: my book, her bag.

PersonPossessive determinerExample
ImyThis is my pencil.
youyourIs this your bag?
hehisTom likes his teacher.
sheherMila reads her book.
ititsThe dog wags its tail.
weourWe love our class.
theytheirThey clean their room.

Careful: its (belonging to it) ≠ it's (= it is).

        At lunchtime we eat in the canteen. I usually have a sandwich and an apple. After lunch, we play in the schoolyard. I like my school because my classmates are nice and we have a lot of fun.
